HUAWEI NOVA

Illustration Brand identity Campaign

An illustrated branding project created for the Huawei Nova, exploring digital space as a kind, creative, and slightly chaotic world of self-expression.

Each person lives in their own world, which is why the screen windows appear as portals into different digital fantasies. Despite the differences between these worlds, the characters continue to interact with one another, creating a shared space of communication.

In the visual style, I wanted to combine elements of retro interfaces, pixel graphics, and Y2K aesthetics. This year, I visited China, and I was amazed by the abundance of bright, bold, and colorful designs throughout the cities. Without even realizing it, Asian pop culture began to influence my creative work.

I wanted to show how this style could be applied within a brand identity, so I developed widget concepts for a screen interface and a smartphone case. On the phone case, the character encounters their digital alter ego. The illustration explores how technology shapes a new aesthetic: digital space is perceived not as cold technology, but as a place of freedom, imagination, and genuine emotion.

Illustration for OZON

Illustration Brand

I had the chance to collaborate with Ozon, a major e-commerce platform! 💙

For this project, I stepped out of my comfort zone and experimented with bright colors that matched their brand identity. I really enjoyed pushing myself in this direction, and I'm happy with how it turned out!

The Ozon team asked me to replace the box with their branded package and adjust the background to match the gray walls of their pickup points.

And now, the best part — my illustration has been printed on the wall of one of Ozon's pickup points! 👩🏻‍🎨💖🤖

It's always exciting to see a digital illustration become part of a real physical space.
